## What the tool can do

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Bulk image creation" icon="seti:image">
		Paste hundreds of prompts. The tool splits the work across many accounts and saves the images automatically. Supports Google Flow, Muse AI and GPT Image.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Subtitles / audio → Video" icon="seti:video">
		Add a subtitle file, audio, video or a YouTube link. The tool transcribes the speech, draws an image for each scene and exports a video.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Script → Video" icon="pencil">
		All you need is a one-sentence idea: AI writes the narration, creates the characters, draws the images and exports the video.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Consistent characters" icon="star">
		Characters keep the same face and look in every scene. Use your own images or let AI draw them.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
