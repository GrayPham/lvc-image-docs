In the tool, click <kbd>💬 Hỗ trợ</kbd> (Support) in the left sidebar or in the bar at the bottom of the window to open the fanpage.

## What to include to get help quickly

1. **Tool version**: the number shown at the bottom of the left sidebar, for example `v1.1.61`.
2. **A screenshot** taken when the error appears.
3. **The steps you took** before the error happened.
4. **Logs**: zip the `logs\` folder and the `app_debug.log` file next to `LVC Image Gen.exe`.

<Aside type="caution" title="Don't send sign-in data">
Do not send the `data\` or `profiles\` folders, the `app_data.json` file, passwords or account cookies. Support never needs these.
</Aside>

Before contacting us, you can check [Common errors](/en/ho-tro/loi-thuong-gap/). Most errors can be fixed yourself in a few minutes.
