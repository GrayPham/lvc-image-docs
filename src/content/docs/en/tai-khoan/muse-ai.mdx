Muse AI accounts are used for the **🎭 Muse AI** model in the image creation tabs. Go to **👤 Tài Khoản → 🎭 Muse AI** (Accounts → Muse AI).

<TutorialVideo id="accounts-muse" />

## Add accounts

<Steps>

1. Click <kbd>🌐 Thêm Tài Khoản (Đăng Nhập Tay)</kbd> (Add Account (Manual Sign-in)), <kbd>🍪 Thêm Bằng Cookie</kbd> (Add by Cookie) or <kbd>📄 Thêm Hàng Loạt</kbd> (Add in Bulk).

2. Fill in the **Email**. Password, invite code and proxy are optional.

3. Choose the **Cách thêm** (Add method):
   - *🌐 Đăng nhập thủ công* (Manual sign-in): the tool opens Firefox and you sign in within 5 minutes.
   - *🍪 Dán cookie* (Paste cookie): paste the muse.ai sign-in cookie (the cookie named `hatch_sess`).
   - *📄 Thêm hàng loạt từ file cookie…* (Add in bulk from cookie file…): choose a cookie file.

4. Click the confirm button. Its text changes with the method you chose, for example *🌐 Thêm & Mở Firefox* (Add & Open Firefox).

5. Turn on the switch in the **Dùng** (Use) column.

</Steps>

## Management

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>🔄 Kiểm Tra Đã Chọn</kbd> (Check Selected) | Checks the signed-in session, up to 3 accounts at a time. |
| <kbd>🔌 Bật Đã Chọn</kbd> / <kbd>⛔ Tắt Đã Chọn</kbd> (Enable Selected / Disable Selected) | Turns many accounts on or off. |
| <kbd>🗑 Xoá Đã Chọn</kbd> (Delete Selected) | Removes the accounts from the table. The Firefox profile folders are kept. |
| <kbd>🚫 Đóng Trình Duyệt</kbd> (Close Browsers) | Closes the Firefox windows the tool opened. |

Right-click an account to *🔐 Đăng nhập lại / làm mới* (Sign in again / refresh), *🍪 Dán cookie mới* (Paste new cookie), *🛡 Đổi proxy…* (Change proxy…) or export and import cookies.

## Status

| Status | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| ✅ Hoạt động (Active) | Ready to use. |
| 🔒 Cần đăng nhập · ⚠️ Chưa đăng nhập (Sign-in needed · Not signed in) | Right-click → *🔐 Đăng nhập lại / làm mới*. |
| ❌ Cookie không hợp lệ (Invalid cookie) | Paste a new cookie. |
| 🌐 Proxy lỗi (Proxy error) | Change the proxy. |

<Aside>
- The Muse model is slower than Google Flow and cannot upscale.
- The number of images running at once equals the number of Muse accounts turned on.
- Muse accepts up to 6 reference images.
- Each Firefox profile does one job at a time. If you see *“Tài khoản đang bận”* (Account is busy), wait for the previous job to finish.
</Aside>
