This page takes you along the shortest path: from a freshly installed tool to your first video.

<TutorialVideo id="quick-start" />

<Steps>

1. **Install the tool and sign in to your license**.
   See [Install the tool](/en/bat-dau/cai-dat/) and [License sign-in](/en/bat-dau/dang-nhap/).

2. **Add at least one Google account**.
   Go to **👤 Tài Khoản → 🎬 Google Flow** (Accounts → Google Flow) → <kbd>🤖 Thêm Tài Khoản Tự Động</kbd> (Add account automatically). Wait until the Trạng thái (Status) column shows **✅ Hoạt động** (Active) and the **Dùng** (Use) switch is on.

3. **Prepare a source**: a `.srt` subtitle file, an audio file, a short video, or a YouTube/TikTok link.

4. **Open 🎞️ Phụ đề/âm thanh → Video** (Subtitles/audio → Video) → <kbd>Mở dự án đang làm →</kbd> (Open current project).

5. Click <kbd>Chọn file…</kbd> (Choose file) or paste a link and click <kbd>Tải về</kbd> (Download).

6. Choose the image **Phong cách** (Style) and **Tỉ lệ ảnh** (Aspect ratio) — landscape for YouTube, portrait for TikTok. Leave the other fields at their defaults.

7. Click <kbd>Bắt đầu</kbd> (Start). The tool runs in order: transcribe → split scenes → create images → export video.

8. Click <kbd>▶ Xem video</kbd> (Watch video) to see the result.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip" title="The first transcription takes a while">
On first use, the tool has to download the speech recognition model (~485 MB). It will be much faster after that.
</Aside>
