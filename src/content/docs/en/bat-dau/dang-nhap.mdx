The first time you open the tool, the **LVC Image Gen - Đăng Nhập** (Sign in) window appears. There are two steps.

<TutorialVideo id="license-login" />

<Steps>

1. **Đăng Nhập Tài Khoản** (Account sign-in): enter the **Tên đăng nhập** (Username) and **Mật khẩu** (Password) provided by LVC Media. Tick *Ghi nhớ thông tin đăng nhập* (Remember sign-in details) to sign in automatically next time. Click <kbd>Đăng Nhập</kbd> (Sign in).

2. **Kích Hoạt Bản Quyền** (License activation): paste your **License Key** (format `XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`) and click <kbd>Kích Hoạt</kbd> (Activate).

3. The *Kích Hoạt Thành Công* (Activation successful) box shows the expiry date and the number of devices allowed. Click OK to enter the tool.

</Steps>

Next time you open the tool, it signs you in automatically if you ticked the remember option.

<Aside type="caution" title="An internet connection is required">
Every time it opens, the tool verifies your license with the server. Without internet, the tool shows *Lỗi Kết Nối Server* (Server connection error) and won't open. Check your connection and open it again.
</Aside>

## Keeping track of your license

- The bar at the bottom of the window always shows the expiry date and the number of days left.
- With fewer than 7 days left, the tool reminds you: *“Bản quyền sắp hết hạn!”* (License expiring soon!).
- When it expires, the tool returns to the activation step with your old key filled in. Contact LVC Media to renew, then enter the new key.

## Signing out

Click <kbd>🚪 Đăng xuất</kbd> (Sign out) in the bar at the bottom of the window. The tool deletes the sign-in session and license saved on your computer, then reopens the sign-in screen.

Signing out does **not** delete your image accounts, projects or videos.

## Common errors

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Đăng nhập thất bại (Sign-in failed) | Check your username and password. |
| Kích hoạt thất bại (Activation failed) | The key is wrong, expired, or has used up its device limit. Contact support. |
| Lỗi Kết Nối Server (Server connection error) | Check your connection, turn off VPN or any network-blocking software, then try again. |
| Session Không Hợp Lệ (Invalid session) | Sign in again manually. |
